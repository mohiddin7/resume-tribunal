## 4. Fixes

1. "Developed real-time basketball video analysis for shot and posture analysis, with 76% accuracy using object detection and image classification with CNN and Yolov7 models." Restore "Developed and implemented." The original claims implementation, and that is the closest this resume comes to shipping something.
2. "Developed and executed 100+ complex SQL queries on Federal and State budgets in Agriculture, Education and Health for reports used to advocate for and facilitate policy change." Restore "departments such as" and "instrumental in." As written, it narrows the scope to three departments and weakens the candidate's stated impact.
3. "Classified news as real or fake using DeBERTa, RoBERTa and DistilBERT; RoBERTa achieved the highest F1-score of 0.9916." Restore "models such as" so it doesn't imply these were the only three tried.

Beyond these, nothing material needs fixing in the wording. No line echoes the job description, and every line would read the same sent to another company. The gap is substance: no production engineering, no LLM or agent work, no second required language, and nothing after Dec 2023. The candidate confirmed nothing more to add, so editing can't close it.

## 5. Claim-defence questions

1. **"The basketball analysis was 'real time' at 76% accuracy. Accuracy of what, on what held-out data, and did it run anywhere beyond a notebook or demo?"** Tests whether "implemented" means deployed and whether the metric is well defined.
2. **"How did a QuickSight dashboard produce a 20% increase in model efficiency? What was efficiency, and what was the baseline?"** Tests the causal link between monitoring and the result, and how it was measured.
3. **"Walk me through the PDF invoice extractor: libraries, how you handled varying layouts, error rate, and who used it after you."** Tests engineering depth and whether it became a tool others adopted, which is the core of this role.
4. **"The 800K-rupee savings and the 45% rise in donor funds: what was your part versus the team's, and how were those attributed to your dashboards and reports?"** Tests ownership and attribution of business outcomes.
5. **"An F1 of 0.9916 on fake-news classification is very high. How did you rule out leakage or dataset artefacts?"** Tests ML rigour and willingness to question an overly good result.
