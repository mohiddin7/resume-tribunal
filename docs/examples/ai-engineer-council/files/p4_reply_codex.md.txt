**5. Claim-defence questions**

- **For the invoice automation, what did you personally build, how were extraction failures handled, who used it, and how was the 80% time reduction measured?**  
  Tests implementation ownership, operational reliability, adoption, and the measurement baseline.

- **What did “76% accuracy” measure in the basketball system, and how do the 14% accuracy improvement and 25% runtime reduction relate to it?**  
  Tests metric definitions, evaluation design, and whether the results form a consistent comparison.

- **For the news classifier, how did you split the data and prevent duplicate articles or source overlap from inflating the 0.9916 F1 score?**  
  Tests evaluation rigor and whether the result generalizes beyond the dataset.

- **In the SQL Server/Tableau work, what was your contribution versus the IT team’s, and what specifically made the system secure and reliable?**  
  Tests the boundary of personal ownership and the technical substance behind broad system claims.

- **How did you establish that the nonprofit dashboards produced annual savings of 800K rupees, and what evidence connected their use to those savings?**  
  Tests business-impact attribution and whether the annual figure was observed, estimated, or projected.
