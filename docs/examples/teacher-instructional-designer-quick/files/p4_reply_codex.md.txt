2. **First impression**

Assuming the top third includes the summary, portfolio project and opening teaching bullets, I see a Charlotte-based biology teacher making a deliberate career change, with graduate education, recent instructional-design coursework, Canvas experience and one relevant healthcare-themed Rise sample.

The qualification requiring two or more years designing instruction for adults **or students** is supported by the teaching work. However:

- **Storyline experience is absent** and explicitly contradicted by the candidate’s account.
- **A reviewable portfolio is missing:** a project is described, but no link is supplied.

Separately, the resume does not demonstrate several responsibilities: Cornerstone publishing and maintenance, WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility, or practical application of ADDIE and Kirkpatrick. Coursework establishes exposure to the models, not applied experience.

4. **Fixes**

1. **“Biology teacher of seven years moving into instructional design. Finished an online Certificate in Instructional Design in June 2026, covering ADDIE and the Kirkpatrick model, and holds an M.Ed. in Curriculum and Instruction. Builds courses in Canvas, video lessons in Camtasia and eLearning in Articulate Rise 360.”** Change the final sentence to distinguish established Canvas and Camtasia work from the single Rise portfolio course.

2. **“Had a nurse check the content”** Change this to “Asked a nurse friend to check the content,” unless the candidate can confirm the review occurred and describe the feedback.

3. **“Raised the AP Biology pass rate from 58% in 2022 to 71% in 2025”** Replace “Raised” with “My AP Biology pass rate increased,” and connect it to the review-practice bullet without asserting that the change caused the increase.

4. **“Hand Hygiene eLearning Module | Certificate portfolio”** Add a working portfolio or course link so the hiring manager can inspect the sample.

5. **“Still use the videos for flipped lessons”** Combine this with the preceding Camtasia bullet, preserving “about 60” and the five-to-ten-minute duration.

6. **“Ran the district's two-hour lab safety training for new science teachers in 2023; about 40 teachers came”** Move this nearer the beginning of the teaching entry because it is the clearest evidence of adult instruction and safety training, while retaining “about 40” and avoiding any unconfirmed claim that the candidate designed it.

7. **“Instructional design: ADDIE and Kirkpatrick model (learned in the certificate), learning objectives, lesson planning, differentiated instruction, classroom management”** Remove classroom management to prioritize relevant design skills, while preserving the qualification that ADDIE and Kirkpatrick were learned in the certificate.

8. **“Sponsor the Science Olympiad club”** Cut this lower-priority line to give the portfolio, curriculum alignment and adult-training evidence more prominence.

The terminology shared with the job description is supported and relevant; I see no company-specific wording that would read oddly in an application elsewhere. Missing Storyline, Cornerstone and accessibility experience cannot be fixed by adding unsupported keywords.

5. **Claim-defence questions**

- **What did you personally design and build in the hand-hygiene course, and what feedback did your nurse friend actually provide?**  
  Tests ownership, the extent of Rise experience and whether content review occurred.

- **How were the 58% and 71% AP pass rates calculated, how many students were included, and what else changed between those cohorts?**  
  Tests comparability, measurement and the limits of attributing the improvement to weekly practice.

- **For the lab-safety workshop, did you create the objectives, materials and activities, deliver an existing session, or do both?**  
  Tests the distinction between instructional design and facilitation, plus the candidate’s role in adult training.

- **In the cells-unit rewrite, which decisions and deliverables were yours, and how did the three teachers agree on objectives and assessment items?**  
  Tests individual contribution, collaboration and alignment between objectives and assessment.

- **Where, if anywhere, did you apply ADDIE, Kirkpatrick or accessibility checks in your portfolio course?**  
  Tests whether coursework translated into practice and whether the candidate can distinguish completed work from theoretical knowledge.
