**4. Fixes**

The material fixes concern emphasis and access to evidence, not inflated claims. I found no misleading job-description copying or company-specific wording that would read oddly elsewhere.

1. **“Seven years teaching biology, building every course in Canvas and about 60 Camtasia video lessons. Finished an Instructional Design certificate in 2026 and built a hand hygiene module in Articulate Rise 360 for a portfolio.”**  
   Rework the summary to foreground the M.Ed., instructional design transition and adult lab-safety workshop, while retaining accurate limits on Rise experience.

2. **“Hand Hygiene Module, Articulate Rise 360 | 2026”**  
   Add a working link to the module or portfolio so the hiring manager can assess the required work sample.

3. **“Built a 20-minute module on hand hygiene for an instructional design portfolio and asked a nurse to check the content”**  
   Add the intended audience, learning objective and assessment approach only if verifiable, while preserving “asked a nurse to check” unless a completed review can be substantiated.

4. **“Ran the district's two-hour lab safety training for new science teachers in 2023, attended by about 40 teachers”**  
   Move this near the top of the experience section because it is the clearest evidence of adult instructor-led training.

5. **“Instructional design: ADDIE and the Kirkpatrick model (certificate courses), learning objectives, lesson planning, differentiated instruction, classroom management”**  
   Remove “classroom management” to give relevant design skills more prominence, keeping the coursework qualifier on ADDIE and Kirkpatrick.

6. **“Develop lesson plans aligned with state standards”**  
   Cut this generic bullet because the cells-unit example already supplies stronger evidence of instructional alignment.

7. **“Sponsor the Science Olympiad club”**  
   Cut this bullet to give more space to the portfolio and relevant training work.

The Storyline gap requires actual experience to resolve; adding a keyword would not fix it.

**5. Claim-defence questions**

- **What did you personally build in the hand-hygiene module, and what feedback, if any, did the nurse provide?**  
  Tests authorship, depth of Rise use and whether the requested content review occurred.

- **How many students took the AP exam in 2022 and 2025, and what else changed between those cohorts?**  
  Tests the reliability of the pass-rate comparison and the limits of attributing it to weekly practice.

- **For the lab-safety workshop, which materials and activities did you design, which did you deliver, and how did you check learning?**  
  Tests whether “ran” includes instructional design, facilitation, coordination or some combination.

- **What was your individual contribution to the cells-unit rewrite, and can you walk through one objective, lesson and corresponding quiz item?**  
  Tests ownership within the team and the quality of instructional alignment.

- **Where did you apply ADDIE in your portfolio project, and what would a Kirkpatrick evaluation plan for that module measure?**  
  Tests whether coursework knowledge translates into practical design and evaluation decisions.
