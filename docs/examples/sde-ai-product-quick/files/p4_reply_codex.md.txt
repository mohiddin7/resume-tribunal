**4. Fixes, up to eight**

1. **“Deep learning engineer who built object detection and tracking models for an autonomous-driving platform, reaching 95% precision and recall.”** Separate tracking from the metric and attach 95% precision and recall only to object detection.

2. **“Machine learning and AI: Deep Learning (CNN, RNN, LSTM, GRU), Machine Learning (Supervised, Unsupervised), Generative AI, LLMs, Prompt Engineering, NLP, MLOps”** Trim this to defensible skills and, if genuine experience exists, substantiate generative AI and MLOps with a concrete project describing the candidate’s implementation, evaluation and deployment work.

3. **“Developed and optimized object detection algorithms with OpenCV, YOLO and Faster R-CNN, achieving 95% precision and recall.”** Specify which model achieved the result, the evaluation dataset and conditions, and whether precision and recall were each 95%.

4. **“Created an image clustering method for segmentation with TensorFlow and PyTorch that cut labeling time by 50% and lowered costs through SageMaker.”** Clarify how this differs from the adjacent 50% annotation claim, identify the measured time baseline, and merge the bullets if they describe the same improvement.

5. **“Built a universal data extraction tool with Python and Selenium that reduced extraction time by 66% for 1,00,000 records.”** Use US number formatting, define the scope of “universal,” and merge with the adjacent three-hours-to-one-hour bullet if it describes the same result.

6. **“System Engineer - Deep Learning Engineer | May 2022 - Aug 2023”** Place the university appointments above this older role, clarify overlapping appointments and the nonprofit role’s employment basis, and add any actual activity since May 2025 because the current timeline ends about 17 months before the review date.

7. **“Published a position paper with a team of 6 at a 2022 workshop on data-centric AI at a major machine-learning conference; recognized among its top 100 accepted papers.”** Provide the paper title, workshop, conference, link and personal contribution, and retain “top 100” only with a precise explanation of whether it was ranked recognition or simply acceptance.

8. **“Created an online examination platform with PHP, HTML, CSS, Bootstrap and SQL where educators build exams and students get immediate scores; recognized as the premier project in its category.”** Name the awarding body and exact recognition or cut the “premier project” clause.

No line conspicuously copies Figma-specific job language or would sound misplaced solely because it was sent to another employer. These edits would improve credibility and readability; they would not, by themselves, establish the missing senior generative AI product experience.

**5. Claim-defence questions**

- **Which object detector achieved 95% precision and recall, on what held-out data and threshold, and what did you personally change?**  
  Tests metric literacy, evaluation validity and individual contribution.

- **Are the two 50% labeling and annotation improvements the same result, and how were time savings and annotation quality measured?**  
  Tests whether the achievements are distinct and whether efficiency preserved quality.

- **What did “dynamic AI model selection” do, which alternatives did you compare, and what happened after the chosen model entered production?**  
  Tests technical depth, experimental judgment and lifecycle ownership.

- **What made the extraction tool “universal,” and were the three-hour and one-hour runs performed on the same 100,000 records under comparable conditions?**  
  Tests scope accuracy, benchmark comparability and robustness.

- **How were the nonprofit’s 20% operational improvement and 25% stakeholder-engagement increase defined, and what evidence connects them to your work?**  
  Tests business measurement, attribution and the candidate’s actual ownership.
