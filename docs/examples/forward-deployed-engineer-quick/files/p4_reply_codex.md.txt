The opening positions the candidate primarily for IT operations and automation. These requirements remain unevidenced:

- **Embedded customer engineering partnerships**, especially discovery, scoping and technical delivery with senior engineers at strategic enterprise customers.
- **Design systems and design-to-code:** component libraries, tokens, documentation and connections between design artifacts and production code.
- **Agentic coding and LLM workflows:** MCP, agent skills, orchestration and contextual evaluation. BERT/CNN work does not establish this experience.

- **Field-to-product impact:** turning customer problems into reusable adoption paths or shipped product improvements.

Python satisfies the language option in the posting; missing TypeScript alone is not a reason to reject.

4. **Fixes — eight priorities**

These improve clarity and evidentiary value; they cannot supply missing experience.

1. **“Technical Specialist on a university IT team. Earlier Machine Learning Engineer who built and deployed a FastAPI service on AWS. MS in Computer Science; AWS Certified Developer Associate.”**  
   Replace the chronological recap with a concise account of the supported Python/API deployment and automation work, avoiding a senior/staff or forward-deployed label that the evidence does not establish.

2. **“- **Cloud:** AWS (IAM, ECS, RDS, S3, Lambda, DynamoDB), Microsoft Azure (in progress)”**  
   Name Azure Logic Apps as the demonstrated Azure tool and leave unfinished study in Certifications with its “in progress” qualifier.

3. **“- Led IT projects for end-user equipment imaging, deployment and lifecycle management, improving operational efficiency by 30% and service quality by 25%.”**  
   Define the two measures, comparison period and project scope, retaining the percentages only if the candidate can explain their calculation.

4. **“- Worked with cross-functional teams to troubleshoot and resolve over 150 complex hardware and software issues, cutting resolution time by 20%.”**  
   Add the counting period and one concrete example of a difficult issue, clarifying the candidate’s contribution while preserving the collaborative attribution.

5. **“- Provided escalation support for technical problems, so 95% of critical systems had minimal downtime (under 2 hours) and fast recovery.”**  
   Clarify which systems the 95% covers and whether “under 2 hours” means per incident or over a reporting period, preserving both qualifiers until the underlying measure is established.

6. **“- Built and deployed a scalable FastAPI service on AWS ECS for real-time predictions, improving deployment efficiency by 30%.”**  
   Define “deployment efficiency” and substantiate “scalable” with the actual workload, deployment architecture and ownership boundaries, adding production users or operational responsibilities only if true.

7. **“- Designed cloud infrastructure with AWS Lambda and DynamoDB to process large-scale data in near real-time.”**  
   Replace “large-scale” and “near real-time” with defensible workload and latency details and explain what this infrastructure did relative to the ECS service.

8. **“- Assessed student assignments for quality and completeness before grading, and graded 50 students on assignments and exams.”**  
   Cut this bullet to give more space to engineering implementation, deployment and outcomes.

5. **Claim-defence questions**

- **For the FastAPI/ECS service, what did you personally implement, who used it, and what happened after deployment?**  
  Tests end-to-end engineering ownership, production exposure and the boundary between individual and team work.

- **What exactly improved by 30% in “deployment efficiency,” and how did you measure the before-and-after result?**  
  Tests whether the strongest software delivery metric has a defined baseline and reproducible calculation.

- **Walk me through one of the 150 complex issues: how did you diagnose it, work with the affected people and verify the fix?**  
  Tests debugging depth, adaptability and technical communication beyond ticket volume.

- **What was the ticket-routing logic, how did PowerShell and Logic Apps interact, and how were permissions, failures and incorrect routes handled?**  
  Tests the depth and reliability of the automation rather than familiarity with tool names.

- **For the image-retrieval system, what baseline produced the 20% accuracy and 50% speed improvements, and how was the million-image dataset used in evaluation?**  
  Tests experimental rigor, metric definitions and the candidate’s own technical contribution.
