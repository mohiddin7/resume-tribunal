4. **Fixes**

There are no material factual corrections, materially understated claims or obvious job-description echoes. These three presentation changes would help:

- **“Wrote BigQuery SQL to split customers with no purchase in a year by what they had bought and how much they spent.”** Combine this with the following result bullet so the analytical contribution and observed outcome form one complete example.
- **“Win-back email conversion rose from 2.1% to 2.5% the next quarter; the email manager wrote a different email per group.”** In the combined example, retain the email manager’s contribution and describe the increase as an observed result, since the material does not establish that a controlled experiment proved causation.
- **“Made a few saved views in BigQuery; sometimes ask ChatGPT to help fix a SQL query.”** Separate the saved-view experience from the AI use, preserving “a few” and “sometimes,” so two distinct capabilities are easier to assess.

These edits can improve clarity, but the missing pricing experience is a substantive qualification gap that wording cannot resolve.

5. **Claim-defence questions**

- **For one of the roughly 12 tests, what did you personally decide, how did you determine sufficient traffic, and how did you reach your recommendation?**  
  Tests analytical ownership and understanding of sample size, uncertainty and experiment interpretation.

- **How was win-back conversion defined, what were the underlying customer counts, and what else changed between the 2.1% and 2.5% measurements?**  
  Tests measurement quality and whether the candidate distinguishes an observed increase from a causal effect.

- **Walk me through your most complex BigQuery query and one saved view: what data did you join, at what grain, and how did you validate the output?**  
  Tests SQL depth and reliability beyond frequency of use.

- **Which decisions did the marketing director make using your Looker dashboards, and how did you investigate unexpected KPI movements?**  
  Tests whether dashboard work led to business analysis and decisions.

- **Describe a SQL problem ChatGPT helped you fix and how you checked its proposed solution.**  
  Tests the actual scope of AI use and the candidate’s ability to verify generated code.
