**4. Fixes**

There are no material factual overclaims or underclaims to correct, and no conspicuous Samsara-specific language copied into the resume. Three presentation changes would help:

- **“Student Technical Support Assistant (IT) | Oct 2025 - Present”** — Move this unchanged entry into an Additional Experience section after the relevant DE/BI experience so the first employment entry demonstrates the target work.
- **“Those frameworks drove a 30% increase in data-driven decision-making across critical business operations.”** — Combine this with the preceding BI-framework bullet so the result has its context in the same bullet.
- **“Implemented AWS VPC for secure data transmission. Network performance improved by 25%.”** — Consider cutting this bullet because the unspecified performance measure and unexplained connection to VPC implementation weaken its persuasive value despite the candidate’s confirmation.

The main screening gaps cannot be repaired through wording using the supplied facts. Adding PySpark, SparkSQL, REST APIs, production reliability responsibilities or a completed degree would be unsupported.

**5. Claim-defence questions**

1. **For the enterprise data lake, which architecture decisions and implementation components did you personally own, and which belonged to other team members?**  
   Tests whether “architected” accurately conveys the candidate’s responsibility and technical depth.

2. **What did “over 10TB” represent—source volume, a migration, stored data or recurring processing—and how did the pipeline handle that scale?**  
   Tests the meaning of the volume claim and the engineering decisions behind it.

3. **How did you use Spark and Airflow, and what happened when a task failed or needed to be rerun?**  
   Tests whether the listed skills demonstrate the practical command required by this role.

4. **What was measured to establish the 40% analytics-efficiency improvement and the 25% data-accuracy improvement, and what were the baselines?**  
   Tests whether the percentages have reproducible definitions and whether the candidate can explain their contribution.

5. **What datasets and regulatory requirements were covered by the “100%” compliance result, and who validated that conclusion?**  
   Tests the scope and evidence behind an absolute, organization-wide claim.
