5. **Claim-defence questions**

- **For the Kafka cluster, what did you personally design and implement, what already existed, and which throughput, partitioning, delivery, and failure-recovery requirements drove your choices?**  
  Tests individual ownership and whether “scalable” reflects concrete system design.

- **Take one claims-processing feature from request to production: who needed it, what did you own, how did you model the data, and what did you monitor after release?**  
  Tests product understanding and end-to-end responsibility.

- **What made the Apache Beam framework “user-friendly,” who used it, and how did their workflow differ before and after it existed?**  
  Tests whether the framework solved a demonstrated user problem and how its benefit was established.

- **For the digital asset management microservices architecture, what was the starting architecture, which service boundaries did you choose, and what tradeoffs or production problems followed?**  
  Tests architectural judgment, personal contribution, and operational experience.

- **What testing ran in Jenkins, what triggered it, which failures blocked release, and what evidence showed that manual testing decreased?**  
  Tests the scope of automation, release-quality standards, and the claimed reduction in manual work.
