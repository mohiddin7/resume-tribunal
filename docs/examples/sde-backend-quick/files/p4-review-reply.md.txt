## 4. Fixes

1. **"Software Engineer with 4+ years of experience in software engineering."**
   - The line repeats itself and undercounts the page's own dates.
   - State a figure the dates support, such as "5+ years, about 4.5 in backend," and name the domains (claims processing, digital asset management).

2. **"Develops backend applications and microservices with Java and Spring Boot, with experience in database management, testing and CI/CD."**
   - This would read identically at any company.
   - Replace it with the strongest concrete claim, such as the claims microservices or the Kafka cluster, plus a real scale figure if the candidate has one.

3. **"Languages: Java, SQL, JavaScript"**
   - The original lists Python, and this job names Python.
   - Restore it if the candidate can say where they used it. Otherwise leave it off.

4. **"Designed and implemented a scalable Kafka Messaging Cluster for distributed publishing across business applications."**
   - "Scalable" without a number echoes the job description and invites doubt.
   - Add what the candidate can verify (number of applications, topics, messages per day) and their own part, or drop "scalable."

5. **"Developed Spring Boot microservices for Claims Processing, with automated and manual testing."**
   - Say what the services did for a claim and add one measured result the candidate can stand behind, such as volume or latency.
   - Cut "with automated and manual testing," which is filler.

6. **"Used Threads, Multithreading, Synchronization and the Collection API for application performance."**
   - This reads as a list of Java basics.
   - Cut it, or replace it with the specific concurrency problem solved and what improved.

7. **"AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate"**
   - Add the year earned. This certification expires after three years.
   - If it has lapsed, mark it as expired or remove it. AWS also no longer appears anywhere else on the page.

8. **"ENGINEERING COLLEGE | Computer Science | May 2020"**
   - Name the degree (for example, B.Tech or B.E.).
   - Use normal case, matching the MS line.

## 5. Claim-defence questions

1. **"On the Kafka cluster, did you stand up and operate the brokers, or build producers and consumers on a platform team's cluster? How many apps published to it, and at what volume?"**
   Tests: the candidate's own part, and whether "scalable" has a number behind it.

2. **"At the enterprise software company you designed a microservices architecture about a year into the role. What existed before, and which decisions were yours?"**
   Tests: the starting point, and whether team-level architecture is being written as individual work.

3. **"Walk me through a claim moving through your services: the data model, the failure modes, and how you knew in production that it was working."**
   Tests: the job's "own problems end-to-end… data models… operability, and ongoing metrics."

4. **"Who uses the Apache Beam framework you created, what did it replace, and how did you measure that it was better?"**
   Tests: whether the "framework" was adopted and has a measured result.

5. **"Your current role starts in Aug 2023 while your MS ran to May 2024, and the Software Engineer III role lasted three months. Were these full-time positions?"**
   Tests: the timeline that counts toward the 7-year requirement.
