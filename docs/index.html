Tailor your resume to a job, with an AI checking the AI

Resume Tribunal is a free, open-source skill for AI assistants. It rewrites your resume for a specific job description, then has an assistant from a different company review it blind, so no AI grades its own writing.

npx skills add mohiddin7/resume-tribunal

Then tell your assistant: "Tailor my resume to this job: [link]". Source and install options on GitHub.

How it works

STEP 1
You give the job, your resume, and a few sentences in your own words about what you did.
STEP 2
Your assistant drafts the resume using only your facts. In full council mode, three assistants from three companies each draft one.
STEP 3
An assistant from another company reviews the draft without knowing who wrote it. Every criticism has to quote the line it is about.
STEP 4
A script checks for ATS problems, page length and common AI writing habits. You confirm every changed claim.
STEP 5
You get the resume, the job requirements it still does not show, and the interview questions its claims will invite.

If you have other AI tools installed, the reviews run by themselves in a few minutes. If not, you paste one prompt into another AI app and paste the reply back.

Why a second AI

AI models favour their own writing. In a 2025 study of resume screening, models preferred resumes they had rewritten over human-written ones 67 to 82 percent of the time (Xu, Li and Jiang). If one AI writes your resume and also judges it, its blind spots go unchecked. Resume Tribunal splits the two jobs.

It does not promise interviews, and no study shows a council of AIs writes better than one strong assistant. What you get is independent review and a resume you can defend line by line.

What it will and will not do

It willIt will not
Use the job's exact names for skills and tools that are true of youCopy sentences from the posting or stuff keywords
Keep to one column, standard headings and plain text that ATS software parsesUse tables, text boxes, images or hidden text
Leave a line without a number when you did not give one, and ask youInvent or round up achievements
Flag em dashes, "not X but Y" phrasing and other AI writing habitsClaim the result is your unaided work

A real run

A fictional operations coordinator applying for an operations analyst job. The run used three assistants from three companies. Employers are described by type, and nothing was edited by hand afterwards.

Questions

How do I tailor my resume to a job description with AI?

Install the skill on the assistant you already use and say "tailor my resume to this job" with the job link and your resume. It drafts from your own facts, gets a blind review from another company's AI, checks the result, and asks you to confirm every claim.

Is the resume ATS-friendly?

Yes. One column, standard headings, plain selectable text and visible links. The bundled PDF builder checks page count and line breaks.

Which assistants does it work with?

Any agent that reads the open Agent Skills format. The blind reviewers can be any assistants from other companies, such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok or Perplexity. None is preferred.

Does my resume get uploaded anywhere?

This project runs no server and never sees your resume. The assistants you choose do, under their own terms. The skill hides your name and contact details before sending text to another assistant, and asks first.

Will it sound like AI wrote it?

It is built from your own wording, and a script flags habits that AI-written resumes share. It still used AI, so if an employer asks or forbids AI help, answer truthfully.

Is it free?

Yes. MIT-licensed, no API keys, no new subscription.
