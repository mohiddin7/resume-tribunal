3. **“Developed a machine learning model for housing prices for a real estate firm, achieving 93% accuracy through data cleaning, testing normalization techniques, validation and feature engineering.”**

   Separate model development from any actual A/B experiment and identify what was tested, retaining “A/B testing” only if that method was used.

   Replace the generic process language with the candidate’s specific analysis and resulting operational action, clarifying the baseline and measurement window while preserving “on a daily average.”

6. **“Developed visualizations that showed a 15% reduction in concession abuse and a 25% improvement in fraud detection accuracy for e-reader digital purchases.”**  
   Define the two metrics and comparison periods while retaining the distinction that the candidate visualized these results rather than necessarily caused them.

7. **“Attained a 100% customer issue resolution rate, leading to two "Team Player" and "Achiever of the Month" awards.”**  
   Cut this lower-relevance support bullet to make room for verified analytical ownership, or specify the denominator, period, and resolution definition if retaining the absolute claim.

8. **“Developed an unsupervised and semi-supervised web attack detection system with real-time monitoring and alerting for distributed processing.”**  
   Restore “unsupervised/semi-supervised” unless both methods are confirmed, and restore the omitted “improved monitoring capabilities by 40%” outcome only if the candidate can define and substantiate that measure.

**5. Claim-defence questions**

- **For the 10% reduction in freight delays, what did you personally analyze or change, what was the baseline, and how did you distinguish your intervention from other operational changes?**  
  Tests individual contribution, measurement quality, and causal reasoning.

- **For the work spanning more than 20 sources, what SQL did you write, how did you reconcile different data grains, and which quality checks did you own?**  
  Tests advanced SQL, data modeling, and practical integration responsibility.

- **What does “93% accuracy” mean for housing-price prediction, and how did you evaluate performance on unseen data against a baseline?**  
  Tests statistical understanding, metric selection, and validation rigor.

- **What exactly was randomized in the delivery-time A/B test, what outcome did you measure, and how did the experiment relate to training the prediction model?**  
  Tests whether the candidate distinguishes experimentation from model evaluation.

- **Who used your Power BI outputs, what decisions changed, how were refreshes maintained, and how did you prioritize competing requests?**  
  Tests dashboard ownership, stakeholder influence, automation, and senior-level prioritization.
