**4. Fixes**

1. **“Analysis and programming: SQL, Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib), R, DBMS”**  
   Replace the generic “DBMS” label with actual database platforms if known, and substantiate SQL and Python/R in experience bullets with the queries, transformations and analyses actually performed.

2. **“Presented findings to cross-functional teams through Excel and Power BI.”**  
   Reconnect this to the supported customer-call analysis and specify the audience, recommendation and resulting action, adding dashboard automation or self-service only if those occurred.

3. **“Reduced freight transportation delays by 10% on a daily average by implementing data analytics for decision-making processes, under tight deadlines in an Agile environment.”**  
   Keep “on a daily average,” but replace the generic process language with the delay measure, comparison period, operational action and candidate’s own contribution.

4. **“Developed visualizations that showed a 15% reduction in concession abuse and a 25% improvement in fraud detection accuracy for e-reader digital purchases.”**  
   Define both measures and their comparison periods, preserving the distinction between visualizing improvements and causing them.

5. **“Developed a machine learning model for housing prices for a real estate firm, achieving 93% accuracy through data cleaning, testing normalization techniques, feature engineering and validation.”**  
   Replace “93% accuracy” with the actual evaluation metric and held-out evaluation setup, or remove the number if its meaning cannot be recovered.

6. **“Led experimental design initiatives for data cleaning, feature engineering and validation.”**  
   Describe the actual experiment and ownership if there was one; otherwise cut this repetitive line, since cleaning and validation alone do not establish experimental design.

7. **“Engineered an ML model through A/B testing to predict delivery duration for food delivery orders; deployed it on AWS and visualized it with Power BI.”**  
   Separate predictive-model development from any genuine A/B experiment and identify what was randomized and measured, removing the A/B label if this was only model comparison.

8. **“Developed an unsupervised/semi-supervised web attack detection system with real-time monitoring and alerting for distributed processing, which improved monitoring capabilities by 40%.”**  
   Replace “monitoring capabilities” with the measured outcome, baseline and evaluation conditions, or cut this lower-relevance project if the 40% claim cannot be explained.

**5. Claim-defence questions**

- **For the 10% reduction in freight delays, what exactly was measured, over which periods, what changed operationally, and which part did you personally own?**  
  Tests measurement quality, attribution and individual analytical ownership.

- **Walk me through your most complex SQL analysis involving the 20-plus sources: what was the data grain, how did you handle joins and duplicates, and how did you validate the output?**  
  Tests whether the listed SQL and data-engineering skills translate into reliable hands-on work.

- **What does “93% accuracy” mean for the housing-price model, and how did you separate training and evaluation data and compare against a baseline?**  
  Tests metric selection, leakage prevention and model-evaluation competence.

- **In the delivery project, what were the A and B treatments, what unit was randomized, and how did you determine whether the outcome differed?**  
  Tests whether “A/B testing” represents a controlled experiment and sound statistical reasoning.

- **For the customer-call reduction, what did you recommend, who acted on it, and how did you prioritize the work against other stakeholder requests?**  
  Tests communication, decision influence and the prioritization expected of a senior analyst.
