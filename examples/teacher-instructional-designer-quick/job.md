Instructional Designer

A regional health system (14 hospitals, 30,000 employees) is growing its clinical education team. You'll design onboarding and compliance training for nurses, techs and support staff. Hybrid, Charlotte, NC.

Responsibilities
- Design eLearning, job aids and instructor-led sessions using ADDIE
- Work with subject matter experts (nurse educators, pharmacists, compliance) to turn content into clear learning objectives
- Build courses in Articulate Storyline 360 and Rise 360
- Publish and maintain courses in our LMS (Cornerstone)
- Measure training effectiveness using the Kirkpatrick model
- Keep courses accessible (WCAG 2.1 AA)

Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in education, instructional design or a related field
- 2+ years designing instruction for adults or students
- Experience with Articulate Storyline and Rise
- A portfolio of work samples
- Experience working with subject matter experts

Preferred
- Healthcare or science background
- Video editing (Camtasia or similar)
- Master's degree
