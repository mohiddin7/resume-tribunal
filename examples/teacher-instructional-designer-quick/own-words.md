I've taught biology for seven years, the last four including AP Biology. I build all my courses in Canvas: modules, quizzes, rubrics, for about 150 students a year. During remote teaching in 2020 I started recording short video lessons in Camtasia, I've made about 60 of them, five to ten minutes each, and I still use them for flipped lessons.

On the curriculum committee I rewrote our 9th grade biology unit on cells with two other teachers, we mapped every lesson to a learning objective and a quiz item. In 2023 I ran the district's lab safety training for new science teachers, a two hour workshop, about 40 teachers came.

My AP Biology pass rate went from 58 percent in 2022 to 71 percent in 2025. I changed how I did review: weekly practice questions in Canvas quizzes with feedback, instead of one big review at the end.

The instructional design certificate was online, six courses, finished in June 2026. We learned ADDIE and Kirkpatrick, and I built a Rise course for my portfolio: a 20 minute module on hand hygiene, I asked a nurse friend to check the content. That's the only Rise course I've built. I've never used Storyline, only watched demos.
