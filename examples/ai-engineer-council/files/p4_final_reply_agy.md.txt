1. `Data Science Masters Candidate (CGPA: 3.95/4) | Dec 2023`
Change "Masters Candidate" to the actual degree awarded (e.g., "Master of Science in Data Science"), as listing candidacy with a December 2023 date nearly three years later in October 2026 implies an unfinished degree or an abandoned program.

Cut "resulting in a 100% increase in user interaction" and specify the web framework or deployment architecture instead, as a doubling metric on a personal student project lacks baseline context and sounds artificial.

3. `- Created AWS QuickSight dashboards to monitor model performance, runtime and computational resources, resulting in a 20% increase in overall model efficiency.`
Clarify that dashboard insights guided specific model or infrastructure adjustments that yielded the 20% gain, as creating a dashboard does not directly optimize model efficiency on its own.

Cut this bullet or reframe it around specific technical assets or proof-of-concept deliverables produced during the internship, as passive shadowing reads as training observation rather than engineering impact.

Rephrase to show that dashboards provided operational visibility that aided decision-makers, rather than attributing direct monetary savings and external stakeholder retention solely to dashboard creation.

### 5. Claim-Defence Questions

1. **Claim:** `- Created AWS QuickSight dashboards to monitor model performance, runtime and computational resources, resulting in a 20% increase in overall model efficiency.`
   - **Question:** "How did you define and measure 'overall model efficiency', what was the baseline metric prior to the dashboard, and what specific technical modifications were made to the models or compute resources to produce that 20% improvement?"
   - *Tests:* Evaluates whether the candidate can substantiate performance metrics with engineering changes versus conflating telemetry monitoring with system optimization.

2. **Claim:** `- Built an interactive web application to detect diseased cotton plants, resulting in a 100% increase in user interaction.`
   - **Question:** "What was the baseline interaction volume before the application was deployed, how was 'user interaction' defined and instrumented, and how many active users did that percentage represent?"
   - *Tests:* Assesses data measurement rigor and tests whether the candidate relies on vanity percentages for unmeasured side projects.

3. **Claim:** `- Designed Tableau dashboards to monitor KPIs and expenditure, resulting in improved budget allocation, annual savings of 800K rupees and enhanced stakeholder retention.`
   - **Question:** "What was your specific personal contribution versus the operational leadership team's actions in realizing the 800K rupees in savings, and what causal link tied donor/stakeholder retention directly to your dashboard implementation?"
   - *Tests:* Distinguishes the candidate's actual individual engineering/reporting contribution from broad organizational business outcomes.

4. **Claim:** `- Automated PDF invoice data extraction to Excel with Python; data entry time fell by 80%.`
   - **Question:** "What libraries and parsing strategies did your Python automation use to handle schema variances or unformatted PDFs, and how was the 80% time reduction formally calculated across the team?"
   - *Tests:* Tests edge-case error handling, pipeline robustness, and measurement validity in workflow automation scripts.

5. **Claim:** `- Designed and tested over 30 ML models using AWS S3 and AWS EC2 to find the best model; accuracy increased by 14% and runtime decreased by 25%.`
   - **Question:** "What model families and hyperparameter search architectures did you evaluate across those 30 iterations, what baseline model were you comparing against, and how did you profile runtime on EC2?"
   - *Tests:* Probes empirical experimentation discipline, understanding of system trade-offs between compute latency and model accuracy, and hands-on depth during the internship.
