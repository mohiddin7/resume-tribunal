**5. Claim-defence questions**

1. **For the PDF invoice automation, what did your Python code do, and how was the 80% reduction in data entry time calculated?**  
   Tests implementation depth and measurement; the supplied material does not establish users or broader system scope.

2. **In the SQL Server and Tableau work, what did you personally implement, what did IT implement, and what made the system secure and reliable?**  
   Tests ownership, technical substance and whether the 70% reporting improvement can be explained.

3. **What did “accuracy” measure in the basketball system, and what comparisons produced the 14% accuracy gain and 25% runtime reduction?**  
   Tests metric definitions and experimental reasoning; the candidate has already identified the accuracy baseline and percentage interpretation as unknown.

4. **How was the news classifier’s 0.9916 F1-score evaluated, including the data split, averaging method and checks for duplicate or overlapping articles?**  
   Tests evaluation quality and leakage awareness; the supplied material does not establish these details.

5. **How did the Tableau dashboards contribute to annual savings of 800K rupees, and who calculated or validated that amount?**  
   Tests the connection between the candidate’s work, stakeholder decisions and the financial result.
