## 1. Conflicts

The drafts did not disagree on any employer, title, date, number, tool or credential. They differed only in wording and placement. The original resume decided each case:

- **Data lake verb:** R says "Built" and M says "Architected". The original says "Architected", so that was kept.
- **BI frameworks verb:** R shortened this to "implementing". The original says "Architected and implemented", so that was restored.
- **Cloud skills:** M lists RDS, Lambda, Lex, SNS and VPC, and R does not. All five appear in the original's experience or project lines, so they were kept.
- **Student role placement:** M places it after the IT company role, and R places it first. It is the current role (Oct 2025 - Present), so it goes first, in reverse-chronological order.

## Open questions

These are for the candidate and are not part of the resume.

1. **Bachelor's degree:** Do you have a completed bachelor's degree? What field was it in, and which institution and year? The posting requires a BA or MS.
2. **Spark:** Did your Glue jobs use PySpark or Spark SQL? Where else did you write Spark code, and at what data volume?
3. **Airflow:** What did you orchestrate with Apache Airflow? How many DAGs, what schedules, and what did they feed?
4. **REST APIs and backend code:** Did you pull data from REST APIs or read backend application code in any role or project?
5. **Data modeling:** Which tables or schemas did you design, for example in Redshift or the data lake? At what scale?
6. **Pipeline reliability:** Did you own monitoring, alerting, data freshness, or failure recovery for the Glue pipelines?
7. **Removed metrics:** How were these three figures measured? Each can go back on the resume if it can be stated concretely.
   - "100% of datasets met regulatory requirements across the organization": Which datasets, which regulations, and what was your part?
   - "30% increase in data-driven decision-making"
   - "25% improvement in network performance" from the VPC
8. **Student Technical Support Assistant:** Is this role part-time? Are there any duties you want listed?
9. **Airline dashboard:** Was this built for the airline as a client, or as coursework on airline data?
10. **Job changes:** Was the move from Business Intelligence Analyst to Data Engineer a promotion within the same employer? Were both roles full-time?
