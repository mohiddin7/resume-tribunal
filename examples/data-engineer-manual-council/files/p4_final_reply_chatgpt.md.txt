**4. Fixes**

1. **“Data Engineer who architected an enterprise data lake and built AWS Glue ETL pipelines on S3, Redshift, and RDS for a national pharmacy chain client. AWS Certified Data Engineer Associate, with a Master of Data Science expected Dec 2026.”**  
   Remove the personal architecture claim and lead with the supported AWS Glue pipeline work and approximately one year in the Data Engineer role.

2. **“Architected an enterprise data lake on AWS S3, Redshift, RDS, and Glue to support storage, processing, analytics, and rapidly changing datasets.”**  
   Describe work on the enterprise data lake without claiming ownership of its architecture.

3. **“Established a data governance framework with data quality checks and compliance measures that raised data accuracy by 25%.”**  
   Describe participation in the governance work rather than personal ownership of establishing the framework, retaining the candidate-confirmed 25% result.

4. **“Developed a serverless chatbot with AWS Lex, Lambda, DynamoDB, and SNS that handled over 500 user queries daily and improved response efficiency by 40%.”**  
   Change “user queries” to “queries” because real-user versus test traffic is unresolved, preserving “over,” the daily period, and the retained 40% figure.

5. **“Implemented an AWS VPC to secure the chatbot's data transmission.”**  
   Remove the unsupported security purpose and retain only the VPC implementation, or cut this low-value bullet.

6. **“Student Technical Support Assistant (IT) | Oct 2025 - Present”**  
   Keep the exact title and dates but place this bare entry under additional experience so the relevant Data Engineer work is encountered first.

7. **“Languages: Python (NumPy, Pandas), SQL, PL/SQL, R, Hive, SAS”**  
   Prioritize Python and SQL, move Hive to the data tools category, and trim less relevant languages to make the intended specialization clearer.

All identified overclaims are covered above. I found no material understatement requiring restoration and no conspicuous borrowing from Samsara’s wording. The three explicitly excluded figures—100% regulatory compliance, 30% increased data-driven decision-making, and 25% network-performance improvement—are correctly absent.

**5. Claim-defence questions**

- **Which parts of the enterprise data lake and governance framework did you personally implement, and who made the design decisions?**  
  Tests individual contribution and whether the ownership verbs are justified.

- **For the 40% analytics-efficiency improvement and 25% data-accuracy improvement, what changed, what was measured, and over what period?**  
  Tests whether the results have defined metrics and defensible attribution.

- **What does “over 10TB” represent, and how did the Glue jobs process that data?**  
  Tests the scale claim, cumulative versus recurring volume, and practical understanding of the execution engine.

- **Where have you used Spark and Airflow, and what code or workflow did you personally build with each?**  
  Tests whether listed skills meet the role’s applied Spark and orchestration requirements.

- **Were the chatbot’s 500-plus daily queries production or test traffic, and what supported the response-efficiency and VPC security claims?**  
  Tests deployment context, performance evidence, and security understanding.
