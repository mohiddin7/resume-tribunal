### 5. Claim-Defence Questions
1. **Question:** "You state that you architected an enterprise data lake on AWS (S3, Redshift, RDS, Glue) for a national pharmacy client during your 1-year tenure as Data Engineer; what was the existing architecture before you began, what specific design decisions and architecture artifacts were your own responsibility versus the broader engineering team, and what specific role did RDS play in that data lake?"
   - *Testing:* Probes whether the candidate independently designed the architecture or merely implemented components of a team architecture, while verifying their understanding of core data lake architecture patterns.
2. **Question:** "In your work optimizing a large-scale data processing pipeline, you note that you improved analytics efficiency by 40%; what was the baseline bottleneck, how specifically did you measure 'analytics efficiency', and what concrete code- or infrastructure-level changes achieved that gain?"
   - *Testing:* Tests hands-on pipeline performance tuning capabilities and whether the 40% metric is tied to a measurable operational benchmark or is an unverified estimate.
3. **Question:** "You credit your data governance framework with improving data accuracy by 25% and ensuring 100% regulatory compliance; how did you define and measure baseline data accuracy mathematically, and what automated validation and auditing mechanisms guaranteed full compliance across all organizational datasets?"
   - *Testing:* Tests the candidate's familiarity with real-world data quality frameworks, compliance tooling, and the validity of their quantitative measurement methodology.
4. **Question:** "When writing ETL scripts to clean, transform, and integrate over 10TB of data, what specific technical challenges did you encounter regarding partitioning, memory management, data skew, and schema evolution in AWS Glue, and how did you resolve them?"
   - *Testing:* Tests whether the candidate has genuine, hands-on big-data engineering expertise handling multi-terabyte datasets at scale.
