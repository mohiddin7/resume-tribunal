The thing I'm proudest of is the win-back emails. Customers who hadn't bought anything in a year were all getting the same email. I wrote the SQL to split them by what they'd bought before and how much they'd spent, and our email manager Ruth Okonkwo wrote a different email for each group. Conversion on those emails went from 2.1% to 2.5% over the next quarter.
