At the freight company I track about 400 shipments a day across 12 carriers. Every Monday I send the ops manager a report on late deliveries. Last year I noticed one carrier was late on almost a third of its loads out of our Dayton terminal, I pulled six months of data in Excel and showed it was their Friday pickups, we moved those loads to a different carrier and on-time delivery for that terminal went from 81 percent to 93. I built the Monday report myself with pivot tables and some Power Query, it used to take the last person half a day and now takes me about 40 minutes. I started learning SQL last year and I write simple queries against our shipment database a few times a week. I trained four new coordinators. At the grocery delivery company I answered about 60 calls and emails a day about late or missing orders and I kept a spreadsheet of the most common causes that my supervisor started using in the weekly meeting.
