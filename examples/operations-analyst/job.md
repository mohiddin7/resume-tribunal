Operations Analyst

About the company: an online home-goods retailer with about 400 employees and two fulfillment centers in the United States.

What you will do:
- Own weekly reporting on order fulfillment, on-time delivery and carrier performance
- Find the causes of late and damaged shipments and work with warehouse and carrier teams to fix them
- Build and maintain dashboards in Power BI and write SQL against the order database
- Run small process experiments in the fulfillment centers and report what changed
- Present findings to the operations director each month

What we are looking for:
- 3+ years in operations, logistics or supply chain
- Strong Excel and working SQL (required)
- Experience with Power BI or Tableau
- Clear written and spoken communication
- Experience with carrier management or warehouse operations is a plus
