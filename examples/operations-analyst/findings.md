# What the council added

## What each seat caught that the others missed

The host
- One draft said the candidate trained coordinators "on shipment tracking and reporting procedures", tracked shipments "to monitor on-time delivery and carrier performance", and wrote SQL "several times a week". None of these is in the candidate's material.
- "Managed daily cash handling operations" enlarges "handled cash". Seat B had preferred that wording.
- One draft reversed the order of events: the candidate noticed the late loads first, then pulled six months of data.

Seat B
- The host's draft rounded one year and ten months of customer service to "2 years".
- The merged resume had dropped the qualifier on SQL in the Summary and Skills. It went back in as "simple" and "(basic)".
- "Cut the time it takes" could be read as the time late deliveries take, not the time the report takes.

Seat C
- The Summary used third-person verbs ("Tracks", "writes") while the bullets used "Track" and "Write".
- The merged resume had lost the starting point of the Dayton story, that the carrier was late on almost a third of its loads. This was not applied, because the user accepted wording changes only.

Caught by more than one seat
- One draft said the candidate moved the loads to another carrier; the candidate said "we moved". (the host, Seat C)
- "A skill started in 2025" made the SQL line read as an apology. (Seat B, Seat C)
- The analysis and its result read better as one accomplishment than as two unrelated bullets. (Seat B, Seat C)

## Rejected as invented or enlarged

- "from 4 hours to 40 minutes": the candidate said "half a day" and "about 40 minutes".
- "Monitor daily shipment integrity and carrier on-time performance" and "monitoring late-delivery exceptions and carrier performance".
- "querying relational shipment databases weekly to extract performance and delivery metrics", and removing "simple" and "(basic)" from the SQL lines.
- "operational reporting adopted by leadership", for a spreadsheet one supervisor used in weekly meetings.
- That the candidate recommended the carrier change. The candidate said only that they presented the finding once.
- "Trained four new coordinators on shipment tracking and reporting procedures".
- "Managed daily cash handling operations".
- "Consistently met performance goals", which is in the original resume but has no evidence behind it, was left out.

## Limits of this run

- The host's own critique and the merge ran in the host's working context, not a fresh one, and the host knew which draft was its own. The host's draft became the base of the merge.
- Seat B ran one tier below the host, the closest its plan offered.
